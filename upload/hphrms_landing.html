One platform, three businesses

Software, manpower, or both — the same login

Most HR systems assume you employ everyone you manage. In India, that assumption breaks the moment you take on contract labour. HPHRMS handles both relationships without pretending they're the same thing.

AI that reads your records — not the internet

Ask in plain language. Get answers from your own data.

Every assistant is scoped to one company's records and one user's permissions. It reads; it does not write. And it will tell you when it doesn't have the number rather than inventing one.

Data access

Your client sees the work. Not your cost sheet.

In manpower supply, the margin is the business. HPHRMS enforces that boundary in the database itself — through row-level security, not through a hidden button. A client cannot reach another company's records even by editing the address bar.
